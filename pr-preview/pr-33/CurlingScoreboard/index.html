Curling Scoreboard

Scoreboards, leagues and bonspiels for curling clubs

Open the App

Open it and keep score

Curling Scoreboard runs in any browser. Open it on whatever device is sitting by the sheet, tap in the score as the ends go by, and that is the whole thing. No install, no account, nothing to set up.

Keeping score is free and accessible to anyone. If you're a curling club looking for a bit more management of your scores, check out our paid features below.

Visit curlingscoreboard.app

Run it across your whole club

A scoreboard paired to every sheet, your leagues and bonspiels planned in it, and the scores on screens around the building.

A scoreboard on every sheet

Pair each sheet's scoreboard once with a six character code. The club portal then shows every sheet at a glance. See what is live, which scoreboards are online, and which need an update. Scoring still works if the internet does not, and syncs when it comes back.

Leagues and real team names

Set up each league with its draw times and teams, or import the whole roster from a CSV. On league nights the scoreboard offers that night's league and puts both team names up on their rock colors, so players can check that whoever set it up got it right.

Bonspiels, start to finish

Plan a spiel on a draw board, or fill one in from a knockout or pools template. Brackets and pool standings work themselves out as results come in, and each sheet's scoreboard offers that sheet's next game with the teams already filled in.

A warm room screen

Every sheet on one TV, sized to fill whatever screen you put it on, to see the overview of all the action. Add your club's name and logo to the title bar. Leave it running for the season.

A score bug for your stream

A transparent overlay you drop into your streaming setup as a browser source. It shows the teams, scores, hammer and end, scales to fit your layout, and hides itself between games.

Scores on your website

A read-only API serves live and finished games, leagues and bonspiels to your club's site. Or have every finished game post itself to a Slack or Discord channel, in a message you write.

For the long version including how pairing works, what the bonspiel templates do, how the displays are set up, and what goes out over the API and webhooks — see the full write-up on the blog.
