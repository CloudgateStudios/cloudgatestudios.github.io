Privacy Policy

Peak Form

Effective 31 October 2026.

Peak Form is made by Cloudgate Studios. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Peak Form on iPhone, iPad or Mac. Peak Form isn't affiliated with or endorsed by TrainingPeaks.

The short version

We don't collect any of your data. Peak Form has no accounts of its own, no servers of ours, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your training data moves directly between your device and your own TrainingPeaks account.

Signing in

You sign in on TrainingPeaks' own login page, shown inside the app. Your password goes to TrainingPeaks, and Peak Form doesn't read or store it. Peak Form keeps only the session TrainingPeaks issues, in the Keychain on that device, and sends it only to TrainingPeaks.

What Peak Form reads and changes in TrainingPeaks

Using your session, Peak Form connects from your device to TrainingPeaks to read your profile and settings, including your name, photo, time zone, thresholds and zones. It also reads your workouts, including recorded routes and other workout data, along with comments, calendar notes, races, and fitness and performance metrics.

Changes you make are saved to your TrainingPeaks account, including workouts, steps, comments, notes, how a workout felt, and thresholds and zones. Your coach and anyone else with access to your TrainingPeaks account can see them. TrainingPeaks handles this data under its own privacy policy.

What stays on your device

Peak Form keeps a copy of your calendar and metrics on your device so it opens quickly and works offline, along with any edits waiting to be sent to TrainingPeaks. Device backups may include waiting edits, widget data and your settings in the app, but not your session.

Apple features

These features use your training data only if you use them. Apple handles the data it receives under its own privacy policy.

  • Apple Watch. Planned workouts are scheduled in the Workout app on your paired Watch.
  • Widgets, Spotlight and notifications. Workout names, summaries, races and fitness numbers can appear in widgets and in search on your device. Notifications are created on your device and can show workout names and your coach's comments, including on your Lock Screen, depending on your settings.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. If you ask about your training, Peak Form gives Siri or Shortcuts the answer.
  • Maps. Routes are drawn on Apple Maps, which loads map data for the area shown. Peak Form doesn't ask for your device's location.
  • Apple Intelligence. Drafting steps from a workout's description uses Apple's on-device model. Peak Form doesn't send the text to us.

Peak Form doesn't read from or write to Apple Health.

Health and fitness data

We don't receive your training data, and Peak Form doesn't use it for advertising or marketing, sell it, or share it with data brokers.

TestFlight

If you test Peak Form through TestFlight, Apple shares information with us so we can run the beta. This can include your name and email address, your device and system version, install and session details, crash reports, and any feedback you send. We use it only to test and improve Peak Form. Apple describes this in its TestFlight privacy information.

Removing your data

Signing out removes your session, the copy of your data, waiting edits, widget and Spotlight data, Peak Form's notifications, and workouts it scheduled on your Watch. Sign out before deleting the app: deleting Peak Form removes its data, but your device may keep the session in the Keychain.

We hold no training data to delete. To access, change or delete what's in your TrainingPeaks account, use TrainingPeaks or contact them. For information we receive through TestFlight, email us at the address below.

Children

Peak Form is for people who already have a TrainingPeaks account, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.

Contacting us

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, you may email privacy@cloudgatestudios.com.
