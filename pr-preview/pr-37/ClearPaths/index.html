Clear Paths

Endless Minesweeper. No guessing.

Coming soon to the App Store

Small clues, and plenty of room to roam

Clear Paths is Minesweeper with no edge to the board and no coin flips. A world goes on as far as you care to wander, and each zone is checked before you play it, so the next safe move can always be worked out from the clues.

Hit a mine and you lose the zone, not the world. It locks, you carry on next door, and it reopens when a zone beside it clears. Come back whenever you like and the board is where you left it.

Each world has one of four difficulties. Pick from five themes or mix your own colors. New to the game? Guided practice walks you through revealing tiles, reading clues and planting flags.

There is no account to make. Your worlds stay on your device and sync through your own private iCloud. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no purchases inside the app.
