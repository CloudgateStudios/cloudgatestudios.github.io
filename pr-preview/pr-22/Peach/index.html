Peach

Live women's pro baseball on your Lock Screen

Coming soon to the App Store

The score, without opening the app

Peach puts a live game on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. It appears at first pitch and updates itself — score, inning, count, and who is on base.

Most nights there is no game on, so the rest of the app answers what you actually want to know in between: the next game, what happened last night, where your team stands, and who is leading the season.

Peach is unofficial. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Women's Pro Baseball League. Team names are used descriptively, and no league logos or marks appear in the app.
