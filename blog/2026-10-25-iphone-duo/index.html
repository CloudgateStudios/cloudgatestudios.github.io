Peach and Bike Share Buddy on iPhone Duo

Both apps now support the iPhone Duo, and make full use of both of its screens.

Peach and Bike Share Buddy both support the iPhone Duo now.

The goal was simple: whichever screen you're using, the app should feel like it was made for it. That means a good experience on the cover screen when the phone is closed, and using all of the extra room when you open it up, not just stretching the phone layout to fill the space.

Bike Share Buddy

Closed, Bike Share Buddy works the way it does on any iPhone: the map fills the screen and the nearest stations sit in a sheet over it, so you can see how many bikes and open docks are around you at a glance.

Open it up and there's room for both. The stations move into a list down the side, and the map gets the rest of the screen. Tap a station in either one and it's picked out in the other.

Peach

Peach's Live Activity works on the Duo just like any other iPhone, keeping the game on your Lock Screen while you get on with something else.

Inside the app, opening the phone gets you the wide layouts. The Season tab shows the full standings table, recent results and what's coming up all on one screen, and Schedule and Leaders spread out the same way. It's the same app, just with room to show you everything without scrolling.

Get them

Both apps are on the App Store.
